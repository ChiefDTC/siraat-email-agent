- Drive pagination for the generic image/ query dies after page 3 (300 results, token rejected). image/jpeg pagination dies after page 1 every time (3 retries, also with mimeType = ...). image/png pagination works but returns overlapping pages. I walked 21 png pages (2,100 raw rows, deduplicated) plus 3 generic pages, 2 jpeg pages, 1 svg query and 14 per-folder queries, then stopped: the remaining pages are older (pre-June 2026) agency ad batches of the same kind. JPEGs outside the two "Statics" Top-200 folders and the MAIN LOGO folder are therefore under-represented; the agency uploads almost exclusively PNG, so the miss is small, but any phone/camera JPG photography the owner uploaded elsewhere would not be in this index.

- Drive folder names: all direct parents of indexed files were resolved via get_file_metadata except 11 deeper grandparent ids that were not needed.
