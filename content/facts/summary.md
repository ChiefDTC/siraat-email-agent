Trust (owner override replaces guidelines)
- "75-year warranty" (support wording: "75-year warranty against defects"). NOT "Lifetime warranty".
- "30-day returns" / "Free 30-day returns" (unused products). NOT "100-day trial".
- "Free shipping on all orders" (worldwide, DDP)

Offer (owner override)
- HI10: 10% off on top of the current sale (Shopify code active, 10% off one-time purchase products)
- October campaign: up to 50% off + 4 free gifts (free shipping $15, Plastic-Free Home E-Book $30, mystery gift $25, weekly chance to win a $450 PFAS water purifier)
- Evergreen baseline per guidelines: 41% off + free mystery gift

Chef video, three questions to ask any titanium brand
1. An independent lab report
2. A physical certificate with the lab's name and a report number
3. A warranty that outlasts you (75 years)

Forbidden: "Lifetime warranty", "100-day trial", "Scratch-proof", "Scientifically proven to retain more nutrients", medical or disease claims, "doctor recommended", attacks on named competitors (HexClad, Caraway, Made In appear in the Notion research; never name them), vague superlatives. Avoid "non-stick" as a headline (welcome-v3 decision); say "natural release" or "food slides right off".

Needs proof before use: any layer thickness in mm (the "0.5 mm" line in the guidelines is a style specimen), "Oven safe to 548°C / 1000°F", "Light Labs 30 of 30 tests passed", the Light Labs report number.
