# 12-Pcs Cookware Set · bezwaren en vragen

Bronnen: content/reviews/objections.csv (400 tickets 7 aug tot 5 okt + 154 lage reviews), Gorgias AI-guidances, Gorgias-tickets gelezen op thema (geen klantgegevens overgenomen). Antwoorden in het Engels, klaar voor mail.

## 1. "Does food stick? Is it really non-stick without a coating?"

- Hoe vaak: Grootste bezwaar: 60 van 400 tickets, 81 van 154 lage Trustpilot-reviews (objections.csv).
- Antwoord: There is no coating, so release comes from heat, not chemistry. Preheat on medium for 2 to 3 minutes, do the water test, add a teaspoon of oil, then let food release on its own. "First heat, then oil, then patience."
- Bewijs: R069, R017, R169; Gorgias 5326403 sectie 7

## 2. "Is it really titanium? What about aluminum, PFAS, nickel?"

- Hoe vaak: 43 van 400 tickets, 28 pre-sale (objections.csv).
- Antwoord: Three layers, and only the titanium touches your food. Pure titanium cooking surface, aluminum core sealed inside for even heat, stainless steel induction base. Tested free from PFAS by Light Labs, report no. 25895. Nooit "nickel-free" beloven.
- Bewijs: R555, R339; Light Labs 25895; Gorgias 5326403 sectie 3

## 3. "Will everything arrive at once?"

- Hoe vaak: Splitzendingen: 48 van 154 lage reviews gaan over levering; ruim 2.000 deelzendingen per maand (PLAYBOOK 5).
- Antwoord: Sets can arrive in more than one parcel, each with its own tracking. Lids sometimes ship separately from the pans.
- Bewijs: R053, R530 (arrived in phases, all works as stated); macro 347467, 357871

## 4. "Is a set worth it over single pans?"

- Hoe vaak: Pre-sale vraag, Gorgias 8566447.
- Antwoord: A set costs less than the same pieces bought one by one, and replaces your coated pans in one move. Noem geen besparingsbedrag tenzij het uit de echte compare-at komt.
- Bewijs: R025, R398 (bought the whole collection); Gorgias 8566447 sectie 4
