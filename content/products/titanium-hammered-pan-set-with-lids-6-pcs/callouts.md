Voor een "Take a closer look"-anatomie (KOP + 2 regels). Alle maten en materialen uit Shopify Admin API, metafield product.details / product.care_use / product.whats_included (opgehaald 7 okt 2026).

1. **THREE SIZES, THREE LIDS**  
   Mini 8", Small 10" and Large 12", each with its own fitted lid.  
   From one egg to a family stir-fry.

2. **HAMMERED TITANIUM SURFACE**  
   0.5 mm of pure titanium is the only thing that touches your food.  
   The hammered dimples cut contact, so food lets go with a little oil.

3. **ALUMINUM CORE**  
   A 1 mm aluminum core spreads heat fast and evenly.  
   Sealed between the layers, it never touches what you cook.

4. **INDUCTION-READY BASE**  
   0.6 mm magnetic stainless steel on the outside.  
   Works on induction, gas, electric and ceramic.

5. **NO COATING. NOTHING TO WEAR OFF.**  
   No PFAS, PFOA, or BPA. Nothing to chip or flake.  
   Tested free from PFAS by Light Labs, report no. 25895.

6. **RIVETED STEEL HANDLE**  
   A long riveted handle that goes from stove to oven.  
   Handles and rivets are covered by the 75-year warranty.
