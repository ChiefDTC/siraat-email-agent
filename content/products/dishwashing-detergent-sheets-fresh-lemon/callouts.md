# Dishwasher Sheets · callouts

1. **PRE-DOSED**
   One sheet, one load. No measuring, no spills.
   Drop it in the dispenser.
2. **HALF A SHEET FOR A LIGHT LOAD**
   Tear it to fit the load.
   Up to 60 washes per pack.
3. **NO PLASTIC FILM**
   No pods wrapped in plastic.
   Phosphate-free and bleach-free.
4. **WORKS BY HAND TOO**
   Dissolve a piece in the sink.
   Grease lifts off your pans.

Vergelijking (tegen pods en poeder): alleen "no plastic film" en "pre-dosed" zijn onderbouwd (PDP-alt). Geen testdata over schoonmaakkracht.
