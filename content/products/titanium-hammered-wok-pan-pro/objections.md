# Wok Pan Pro · bezwaren en vragen

Bronnen: content/reviews/objections.csv (400 tickets 7 aug tot 5 okt + 154 lage reviews), Gorgias AI-guidances, Gorgias-tickets gelezen op thema (geen klantgegevens overgenomen). Antwoorden in het Engels, klaar voor mail.

## 1. "Does food stick? Is it really non-stick without a coating?"

- Hoe vaak: Grootste bezwaar: 60 van 400 tickets, 81 van 154 lage Trustpilot-reviews (objections.csv).
- Antwoord: There is no coating, so release comes from heat, not chemistry. Preheat on medium for 2 to 3 minutes, do the water test, add a teaspoon of oil, then let food release on its own. "First heat, then oil, then patience."
- Bewijs: R069, R017, R169; Gorgias 5326403 sectie 7

## 2. "Does it work on induction, glass top, in the oven?"

- Hoe vaak: 23 van 400 tickets, 13 pre-sale.
- Antwoord: Yes. The magnetic stainless base works on induction, gas, electric and ceramic, and the pan is oven safe. Use medium heat: medium on titanium works like high on a normal pan.
- Bewijs: R543 (induction hob, low heat); PDP; macro 247042

## 3. "How do I clean it? It has brown spots or a rainbow tint."

- Hoe vaak: 58 van 400 tickets; regenboogtint komt geregeld terug in tickets (zoekterm "rainbow", aug tot sep 2026).
- Antwoord: Warm water and a soft sponge while the pan is still warm. Brown or rainbow tint is heat discoloration, harmless and cosmetic; a baking soda paste lifts it. Dishwasher safe when you are short on time.
- Bewijs: R518, R553; Gorgias 5326403 sectie 8, 5302143

## 4. "Is a lid included? Which lid fits?"

- Hoe vaak: 41 van 400 tickets, 16 pre-sale; geregeld verkeerde deksels in tickets (sept 2026).
- Antwoord: Lids are sold separately in 304 stainless steel, unless the product page says included. Pick the lid with the same diameter as your pan: Mini 20 cm, Small 26 cm, Standard 28 cm, Large 30 cm.
- Bewijs: Gorgias 8566447 sectie 4; R044 (4 sterren, "a must have")

## 5. "Is the Mini a real wok shape?"

- Hoe vaak: Review R162 (juli 2026): klant dacht verkeerd item te hebben.
- Antwoord: The Mini 24 cm is a compact, shallower shape. For classic wok tossing, choose Small 10" (26 cm) or larger.
- Bewijs: R162; PDP mist Mini-specs
