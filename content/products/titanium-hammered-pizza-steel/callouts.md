# Pizza Steel · callouts

1. **HAMMERED SURFACE**
   The hammered pattern lets air under the dough.
   Pizza and bread release after baking (PDP).
2. **NO COATING, NO SEASONING**
   Nothing to burn off, nothing to maintain.
   Preheat it well and bake.
3. **SIDE HANDLES**
   Two handles to lift it in and out of the oven.
   Works in home ovens, pizza ovens and on the grill (PDP).
4. **DISHWASHER SAFE**
   Let it cool, then into the dishwasher.
   Or warm water and a little soap.

Vergelijking alleen tegen stone: stone kan barsten en neemt geur op (PDP-claim van Siraat, algemeen bekend). Geen eigen testdata.
