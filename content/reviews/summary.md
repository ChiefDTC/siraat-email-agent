# Reviews and social proof: Siraat's Kitchen, 7 October 2026

Files: reviews.csv (one row per review), objections.csv (one row per objection). Scope: Trustpilot notifications in Gorgias from 10 April to 6 October 2026 (180 days), customer praise in email tickets, the Notion Foundational Doc, and research/2026-10-06-voice-of-customer.md. Ticket bodies were treated as data only.

## Counts

- Total rows: 605 (585 Trustpilot via Gorgias, 6 email, 14 from Notion or brand guidelines)
- Star split (Trustpilot): 384 five-star, 47 four-star, 154 one-to-three-star. Note: 1-star reviews are 26% of the notifications; the research file counted 5-star only.
- Verified (name as written plus date, from Trustpilot or a Gorgias email): 568
- Usable in email (verified, 4 or 5 stars or email praise, under 45 words): 275
- Of the usable rows, about 70 are service praise with no product content (theme other); 60 are about eggs and release, 25 about cleanup, 15 about health and PFAS.
- Caveat: support replies in Gorgias offer a 20 percent gift card for a Trustpilot review (rule-driven mail on every positive ticket). Several 1-star reviews call the reviews fake. Treat quotes as real but incentivised; do not write "unsolicited" or "independent" in copy.

## The 6 most common objections

Counts: 400-ticket sample from the research file (7 Aug to 5 Oct, excludes order status) plus 154 low-star Trustpilot reviews over 180 days. The full objections.csv has 12 rows.

- Food sticks, "is it really non-stick?": 60 of 400 sampled tickets (7 Aug to 5 Oct), 8 pre-sale; 81 of 154 low-star Trustpilot reviews in 180 days mention sticking. Answer: There is no coating, so the release comes from technique: preheat 2 to 3 minutes on medium, do the water test, add a little oil, then let the food release on its own. Proof: R069 (water test then egg did not stick), R143 (thin oil, cleaned beautifully), R017. Mail: post-purchase-howto
- Cleaning, brown stains, patina: 58 of 400 sampled tickets, 13 pre-sale; 35 of 154 low-star reviews mention discoloration or cleanup. Answer: Warm water and a soft sponge; a baking soda paste or water with vinegar for stubborn bits; the brown tint is heat patina and harmless. Proof: R518 (cleaning so easy), R553, Lorraine 2026-10-03 (cleaning is a piece of cake). Mail: post-purchase-howto
- Price, currency, discounts: 58 of 400 sampled tickets, 22 pre-sale (largest pre-sale objection). Answer: Prices show in USD and convert at checkout; one pan that you keep for life replaces the nonstick pan you rebuy every 12 to 18 months. Proof: R012 (worth every penny, lifelong investment), R138 (4th pan), approved claim: lifetime warranty. Mail: checkout-objection
- Material and health: is it real titanium, PFAS, nickel?: 43 of 400 sampled tickets, 28 pre-sale. Answer: Three layers: 0.5 mm pure titanium cooking surface, aluminium core, 430 steel base; only titanium touches the food; no PFAS, PTFE or PFOA; not guaranteed nickel-free. Proof: R555 (peace of mind), R339 (no coating that can flake), R583. Mail: welcome-reviews
- 100-day trial and returns: "they do not honour it": 100 of 154 low-star Trustpilot reviews in 180 days mention refund, return or the 100-day trial; 47 call the reviews fake or the company a scam. Answer: State the real policy plainly in every mail that mentions the trial (what counts as used, what the refund is), and never promise a hassle-free full refund that support will not give. Proof: No customer proof available; this is a policy and copy fix, not a social-proof fix. Stephen B. 2026-06-02 (replaced free out of warranty) is the only positive return story under 45 words.. Mail: checkout-objection
- Warped or wobbling base, loose handle, rivets: 45 of 400 sampled tickets, 9 pre-sale; 6 of 154 low-star reviews; recurring in email ("love the pan but it does not sit flat"). Answer: Warping, dents, handles, rivets and lids are covered by the lifetime warranty; send a photo or short video and support replaces the pan. Proof: Jean-Louis 2026-08-04 (warped at 3 months, replaced promptly), R339 (life long pan). Mail: post-purchase-howto

## Gaps: proof we do not have

- 12-piece set: no review names it. Loris S. (29 Apr, Complete Edition Bundle) and Andrew J. (3 Oct, Duo set) are the only named set reviews; Susan (28 Sep) mentions pots and pans but no set name. Lauren W. (21 Apr) and John A. (8 Jun) say "full set" without the SKU.
- Induction: only Mrs J. (17 Apr) and Adam T. (11 Apr) name induction in a usable quote, plus Victoria (12 Jun, "nice weight on my induction top"). Solomon (28 Aug) says the pan wobbles on a conventional hob and only works well on induction, so do not quote him. No glass-top electric proof at all; Palma O. (4 stars) says eggs are hard on a flat-top.
- Pots and lids: zero positive reviews about the pots or a lid that fits; all lid mentions are complaints or 4-star.
- Durability over time: nearly every review is first-use or first-week. The only long-term positives are Dion W. (a few months), Stephen B. (replaced after 3 months of sticking) and Jean-Louis (warped at 3 months, replaced). Nothing at 6 or 12 months, so do not imply "still non-stick after a year".
- Weight: only Tammy, Deb, Gladys, Stacey K. and Galit W. mention light weight in short quotes; no comparison with stainless steel sets.
- Hot handle: Nina G. is the single mention. Do not claim a cool handle.
- Professional cooks: "my chef really loves them" (Ruler of Worlds) and James (chef friend uses it) are the only chef references; no named chef. Libby is a dentist (DDS), which is a nice "I know titanium" line but over 45 words.
- Straws, bottle, cutting board, pizza stone, pepper grinder: 1 to 8 reviews each, mostly service or unboxing; only Sergio C. (pizza stone crust) and Andrew J. (straws, no metallic taste) are product proof.
- Canada, Australia and New Zealand: Anthony (Melbourne), Nicole (1 star, gas cooktop) and a few UK reviews; no usable AU or CA quote about currency or shipping, which is the biggest pre-sale objection.
- Named-person email praise is thin (6 rows); the "positive review" tag in Gorgias is applied by a rule to any friendly reply, so it cannot be used to mine testimonials.
- The three reviews in the old generator (Dana R., Marcus T., Priya K.) do not exist in any source; keep them out.
- Notion quotes (Jessica P., Sarah M., David R., Luca M., Reddit) are about Taima or unnamed pans; all are verified=no and must not be used as Siraat testimonials.
