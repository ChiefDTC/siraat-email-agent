YOUR FIRST WEEK WITH TITANIUM
What to expect, step by step
1 The day it arrives
Unbox, wash once with warm soapy water, dry. No seasoning needed.
2 First cook: heat, oil, food
Medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes. A drop of water should bead up and glide. Then a teaspoon of oil, then the food. Cold metal grabs your food, hot metal lets it go.
3 Week 1
Start with eggs. Let food sit until it lets go on its own. If it sticks, the pan was not hot enough yet. Give it another minute next time.
4 Month 1
You stop thinking about it. Metal spatula, dishwasher, oven: all fine. There is no coating to protect.
