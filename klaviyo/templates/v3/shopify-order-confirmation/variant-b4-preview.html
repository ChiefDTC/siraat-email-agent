YOUR FIRST WEEK WITH TITANIUM
Heat, oil, food. In that order.
One tip from me before it arrives. Titanium has no coating, so food lets go because of heat, not chemistry. Cold metal grabs your food, hot metal lets it go. Skip the heat and it sticks; give it one more minute next time.
1Heat
Medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes. A drop of water should bead up and glide.
2Oil
A teaspoon of oil. Swirl it around.
3Food
Lay it in and leave it until it lets go on its own. Start with eggs.
