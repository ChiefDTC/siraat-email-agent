If your box has arrived, there’s a white pack in it that says Dishwasher Sheets. That’s your gift. Here’s all there is to know.

THREE WAYS TO USE THEM
1A full load. Put one sheet in the dispenser, the same flap you’d use for a pod. Close it and run your usual cycle.
2A light load, or two drawers. Tear a sheet in half. That’s how 30 sheets stretch to 60 washes.
3By hand. Dissolve a piece in warm water in the sink. Grease lifts off your pans.

Why sheets and not pods? Most pods come wrapped in a film that dissolves in your machine. These have no plastic film at all, and they’re phosphate-free and bleach-free.

Your pan can go in with them. It’s dishwasher safe. One thing for the longest life: let it cool first, and never rinse a hot pan with cold water.
