First cook, honest tip
There’s no coating on your pan, so release comes from heat and timing. Medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes, flick in a drop of water, and wait until it forms one bead that glides. Then a teaspoon of butter or oil. Skip the preheat, and the egg sticks.
