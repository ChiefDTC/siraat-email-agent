SIX-MONTH CHECK
Three things worth knowing
A brown tint? That is heat, not damage.
It is carbonized oil from high heat. A paste of baking soda and water with a soft sponge takes it off.
Sticking more than before? Back to medium.
Preheat 2 to 3 minutes on medium, never high. Water drop test, a teaspoon of oil, then let food release.
A dent, a warp, a loose handle?
That is what the 75-year warranty is for. Send a photo to support@siraatskitchen.com and we take care of it.
