SIX-MONTH CHECK
Three short answers
A brown tint? That is carbonized oil.
Not damage, and not a defect. A paste of baking soda and water with a soft sponge takes it off.
Sticking more than before? Back to medium.
Preheat 2 to 3 minutes on medium. The water drop should form a ball and glide. Then a teaspoon of oil.
A dent, a warp, a loose handle?
