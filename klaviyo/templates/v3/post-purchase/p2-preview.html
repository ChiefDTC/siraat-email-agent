Five steps, straight from our chef.

Hi Sarah, your pan arrived. There is no coating on it, so the release comes from heat and timing, not chemicals. Here is the first cook, step by step.

THE CHEF'S FIRST COOK
1
Preheat on medium, 2 to 3 minutes
Medium on titanium works like high on a normal pan. You never need to go higher.
2
Do the water test
Flick in a few drops. When they bead up and skate instead of sizzling away, it is ready.
3
Add a thin layer of oil
First heat, then oil. Always into a hot pan.
4
Eggs: turn the heat down to low
Give it a moment. Once the white sets, it lets go by itself, and then it slides.
5
Meat and fish: lay it down, then leave it
It holds on at first, then forms a crust and releases on its own. Moving it early is what makes it stick.
IF SOMETHING STICKS
A sticky pan is not a ruined pan
Mix baking soda with a little water, rub it over the surface with a soft sponge and rinse. Back to day one. A blue or gold shimmer after high heat is harmless heat tint.
