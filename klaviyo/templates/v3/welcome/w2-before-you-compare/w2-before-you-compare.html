Before you start comparing...
I'm the founder of Siraat, and there's something I want you to know. I genuinely care about you more than you could ever imagine.
Years ago, I was working as a chef in a restaurant. Every single day, I was serving people the meals they wanted. Healthy preparations, fresh ingredients, incredible flavors. But something just didn't feel right. I was putting so much love and effort into preparing healthy meals... But what was I cooking them in? Pans with coatings and materials I didn't feel comfortable using. And the worst part? I knew so many people were doing exactly the same thing without ever questioning it. I couldn't accept that anymore.
So one day, I threw off my apron. I walked away from being a chef and started developing something I believed could make a real difference. Not in WHAT we cook. Not in how our food tastes. But in WHAT we cook IN. That's how Siraat started. I spent years developing the Siraat Titanium Hammered Pan. And believe me, it wasn't easy. Years of hard work. Big financial losses. Countless setbacks. Moments when I genuinely questioned whether I would ever make it. But I refused to give up. And eventually, we made it. In November 2024, we finally started bringing our pans to the public. I can't describe how that felt.
But there's something nobody really prepares you for when you start becoming successful. The copycats. And that's exactly what happened to Siraat. Today, we've seen hundreds of lookalikes appear. Some are even doing bigger numbers than us. And what hurts me the most? All of them are selling pans they claim are made with real titanium when that simply isn't what they're delivering. They're misleading people who are just trying to make a better choice for themselves and their families. And that's what makes me angry. Because that's exactly why I started Siraat in the first place.
Now listen. This is NOT a sales pitch. I'm not writing this email to convince you to buy our pan. Don't buy it. We don't even have enough stock to serve everyone. Titanium is a special material, and we can't just produce unlimited quantities overnight. But there's something I really, really want to tell you.
PLEASE. Don't start comparing Siraat with every other so-called "brand" you see online. Even if their pans look EXACTLY the same. Even if their websites look beautiful. Even if they claim to offer exactly what we offer. That doesn't mean you're getting the same product. And please, be careful with those comparison websites. You know the ones I'm talking about. The blogs that look like independent third-party reviews. The articles comparing "the 5 best titanium pans." The websites that seem to have done all the research for you. Sometimes, the people behind those pages are the very same people trying to sell you the product. Fake reviews. Misleading comparisons. Beautiful marketing. Anything to convince you to spend your money.
And that genuinely upsets me. Because at the end of the day, it's YOUR money. YOUR kitchen. YOUR family. And YOUR trust.
So please, remember one thing. If you don't buy a Siraat pan... DON'T BUY ANY. Seriously. Stick to what you have right now. I'd rather you keep cooking with your current pan than waste your hard-earned money on a copy that might not be any better. I'm not saying this because I want your money. I'm saying this because I spent years of my life trying to create something I truly believe in. And watching people get misled by products that only look the same... It hurts. More than you might imagine.
I started Siraat because I cared about what people were cooking in. And I still do. Whether you ever become a Siraat customer or not.
Please take care of yourself. And please, don't believe everything you see online.
From the bottom of my heart,

The Founder of Siraat
