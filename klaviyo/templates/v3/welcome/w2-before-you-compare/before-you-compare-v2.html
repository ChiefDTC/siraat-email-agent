Before you start comparing...
I'm Benjamin, one of the founders of Siraat. Before you compare pans, there's something I want you to know.
We didn't start with pans. We started with titanium cutting boards, because we wanted one material in the kitchen we never had to worry about. Pure titanium. No coating that can wear off.
The pan came next, and it took far longer than we hoped. Prototypes that didn't work. Money we lost. Moments we almost stopped. On December 20, 2024, we finally shipped our first orders. Since then, more than 100,000 people have chosen Siraat. I still can't quite believe that.
But then something happened that nobody prepares you for. Lookalikes. Pans with the same hammered pattern, the same kind of photos, sometimes even the same words.
Here is what worries me. Looking the same is not the same as being the same. "Titanium" can mean a lot of things on a product page. Sometimes it's a nonstick coating with titanium particles mixed in. That coating wears off, just like any other. Ours is a pure titanium cooking surface, with no coating at all.
So if you compare, and you should, ask every brand the same three questions:
1. Can I see the lab report, with a report number?
2. Is the cooking surface pure titanium, or a coating?
3. What does the warranty cover, and for how long?
Our answers: lab report no. 25895, tested for 31 PFAS compounds, every one below the detection limit. A pure titanium cooking surface, no coating. And a 75-year warranty.
If a brand can't answer all three, keep the pan you have now. Honestly, I'd rather you cook with your current pan than spend your money on one that only looks right.
Take your time. And if you want to ask anything about our pans, just reply. Our team will help.
Take care,

Benjamin
Founder, Siraat's Kitchen
