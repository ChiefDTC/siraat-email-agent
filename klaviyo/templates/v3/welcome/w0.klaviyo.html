FIRST USE
Three steps, one egg.
STEP 1
Heat first
Medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes, before anything goes in. Never on high.
STEP 2
Do the water test
A drop of water should form a ball and glide. Evaporates at once? Too hot. Sits still? Wait.
STEP 3
Then oil, then the egg
A teaspoon of oil, 10 to 15 seconds until it shines, then the egg. Let it release by itself.
The first time, use a thin layer of oil. Sandi did: “it definitely needed just a thin covering of oil, and it cleaned up beautifully.”
