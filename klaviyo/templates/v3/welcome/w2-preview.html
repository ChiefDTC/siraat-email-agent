Hi Sarah,

It’s Benjamin. I started Siraat’s Kitchen, and you joined us yesterday, so I wanted to write to you myself.

Two years ago I started reading about PFAS, the “forever chemicals” used in a lot of coated cookware. The more I read, the more it bothered me how normal they had become in our kitchens. I wanted to make switching to non-toxic cookware easy.

We started with titanium cutting boards. Then we made the pan. A cutting board is nice, but in the end you cook in a pan, so the pan mattered just as much.

The idea is simple to say: a titanium cooking surface, a hammered pattern so less food touches the metal, and nothing on it to wear off. Our first orders shipped on December 20, 2024. Getting it right took six months of testing. We could have sold stainless steel, which would have been easier, but it does not cook like titanium with a hammered pattern.

I’ll be honest about one thing. A pan with no coating cooks a little differently. Heat it on medium for 2 to 3 minutes, add a little oil, and the egg slides. Skip the heat, and it sticks. That is the difference between loving this pan and sending it back.

Look-alikes have appeared since. You will recognize the hammered pattern. Ask them for a lab report. Ours is Light Labs report no. 25895: 31 PFAS compounds tested, every one below the detection limit.

If you have a question, reply and our team will help.

Benjamin
Founder, Siraat’s Kitchen
