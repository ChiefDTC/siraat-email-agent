Before you open it
Will food stick?
Not when the pan is hot. Medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes, a teaspoon of oil, and eggs slide. Skip the heat, and it sticks.
Does it work on induction?
Yes. Induction, gas, electric and ceramic. Dishwasher safe, metal utensils welcome.
