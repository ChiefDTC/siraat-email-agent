TUESDAY
Maple sweet potato rounds
Pan Pro Large · 20 minutes
You need
 
2 large sweet potatoes
 
olive oil
 
2 tablespoons maple syrup
 
flaky salt
Method
1
Slice the sweet potatoes into rounds about 3/8 inch (1 cm) thick.
2
Heat the Pan Pro Large on medium for 2 to 3 minutes. Flick in a drop of water. When it balls up and glides, add a thin layer of olive oil.
3
Lay the rounds flat and leave them alone for 4 minutes. Cold metal grabs your food, hot metal lets it go.
4
Flip. Another 4 minutes, until they're golden on both sides and soft in the middle.
5
Drizzle with maple syrup. Salt. Done.
Make ahead: into the fridge in a covered dish. On Thursday, 8 minutes in a low oven.
One honest note: if a round sticks, the pan wasn't hot yet. Give the next rounds another minute.

WEDNESDAY
Green beans with shallots and almonds
Deep Pan Pro · 10 minutes
You need
 
1 lb green beans
 
2 shallots
 
a knob of butter
 
half a lemon
 
a handful of toasted almond slivers
Method
1
Blanch the beans for 3 minutes in salted boiling water. Drain and leave them in the colander.
2
Slice the shallots and soften them in a little butter in the Deep Pan Pro over medium-low.
3
Beans back in. Toss.
4
Squeeze of lemon, almonds on top.
Make ahead: fridge. On Thursday, back into the Deep Pan Pro on the lowest heat while everything else rests. They'll hold there for 30 minutes.
Why the Deep Pan: the higher sides mean you can toss a full batch without beans going over the edge.
