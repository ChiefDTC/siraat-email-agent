How to cook on titanium
Watch the water dance.
Nothing sticks when the heat is right.
Benjamin here. The question I get most from people who haven't bought yet: does titanium really work, or does everything stick?
It works. It just asks for one thing a coated pan never did. Heat first, food second. Four steps, and the first time takes about three minutes longer than you're used to.

1
Heat it on medium
Put the empty pan on medium heat and give it 2 to 3 minutes. Not high. Titanium spreads the heat, it just needs a moment to get there.
2
The water drop test
Flick in a few drops of water, like the photo at the top. If they hiss and vanish, wait a little longer. When they pull into round beads and glide across the surface, the pan is ready.
3
Then the oil
About a teaspoon of oil or a knob of butter. Tilt the pan so it runs into the hammered pattern.
4
Lay it down. Don't move it.
Put the food in and leave it alone. Cold metal grabs your food, hot metal lets it go. When it's ready to turn, it lets go by itself.
One honest note: if something sticks, the pan wasn't hot yet. Give the next one another minute.

After dinner
There's no coating on it, so there's nothing to protect. Metal spatulas are fine. Warm water and a sponge, and you clean up in under a minute. Or put it straight in the dishwasher.
