Two pans, one dinner
Crispy chicken, lemon orzo.
Pan Pro + Deep Pan Pro · 40 minutes
Benjamin here. By now you've probably done eggs, maybe a steak. This one uses two pans at once. The Pan Pro crisps the chicken while the Deep Pan Pro turns orzo creamy, and they finish within a minute of each other.
Leave the chicken alone. When the skin is ready, it lets go on its own.
 
The recipe
Chicken thighs, skin side down
Then the orzo goes on next to it
Serves 4 · 40 minutes
For the chicken · Pan Pro
 
6 boneless, skin-on chicken thighs
 
salt and black pepper
 
1 teaspoon olive oil
 
1 lemon, halved
For the orzo · Deep Pan Pro
 
1 tablespoon butter
 
2 garlic cloves, sliced
 
300 g orzo
 
1 litre hot chicken stock
 
2 big handfuls of baby spinach
 
40 g grated parmesan
 
zest and juice of 1 lemon
Method
1
Pat the thighs dry and salt both sides. Dry skin is crisp skin.
2
Pan Pro on medium for 2 to 3 minutes. Flick in a drop of water. When it balls up and glides, add the oil.
3
Thighs in, skin side down. Leave them alone for 12 minutes. Cold metal grabs your food, hot metal lets it go.
4
Deep Pan Pro on medium, 5 minutes after the chicken went in: butter and garlic for a minute, then the orzo. Stir until it smells toasty.
5
Pour in all the hot stock. Simmer 10 minutes, stirring every minute or so, until the orzo is tender and creamy.
6
Flip the chicken. Lemon halves in next to it, cut side down. 6 more minutes, until cooked through (74°C inside).
7
Spinach, parmesan, zest and lemon juice into the orzo. Stir until the spinach wilts. Chicken on top, seared lemon squeezed over.
The timing: chicken first, orzo 5 minutes later. Both finish within a minute of each other, and both pans go in the dishwasher.
One honest note: if the skin sticks when you try to lift it, it isn't ready. Give it two more minutes and it lets go on its own.
Why the Deep Pan Pro for the orzo: the higher sides let the stock simmer without spitting over the edge, and there is room to stir in the spinach.
