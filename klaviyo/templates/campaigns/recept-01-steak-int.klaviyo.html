THE RECIPE
Steak with a brown butter mushroom pan sauce. A real crust outside, pink inside, and a sauce made from everything the steak leaves in the pan.
Serves 2 · 25 minutes
 
Ingredients
FOR THE STEAK
2 rib eye or sirloin (strip) steaks, about 2.5 cm thick
Salt and black pepper
1 tsp neutral oil
3 tbsp butter
1 garlic clove, lightly crushed
2 sprigs thyme or rosemary
FOR THE SAUCE
250 g (9 oz) mixed mushrooms, torn
1 shallot, finely chopped
120 ml (1/2 cup) beef stock
1 tsp Dijon mustard
A splash of cream, if you like
Flaky salt, to finish
 
Method
Prep the steaks. Out of the fridge 30 minutes early. Pat dry, salt both sides.
Heat your pan to medium. 2 to 3 minutes, until a drop of water beads and glides. Add the oil.
Sear the steaks. 3 to 4 minutes, hands off, until they let go. Turn, 3 more for medium rare.
Baste with butter. 2 tbsp butter, garlic and herbs. Spoon it over until nutty brown. Let the steaks rest.
Brown the mushrooms. Same pan, one layer, 3 minutes before you stir. Shallot for the last minute.
Make the sauce. Pour in the stock and scrape with a metal spatula. The brown bits become your sauce.
Finish it. Bubble 2 to 3 minutes. Off the heat, stir in the mustard, last butter, cream and board juices.
Serve from the pan. Slice the steaks, lay them back in and spoon the sauce over.
Tip: Steak thicker than 4 cm? After searing, slide the pan into a 200°C (400°F) oven for 4 to 6 minutes. The handle comes out hot too, so grab a towel.
 
That brown layer in step 6 has a name: fond. A coated pan is made to let go of everything, so there’s little of it left to work with. On titanium it stays put, and it’s the best part of dinner.
