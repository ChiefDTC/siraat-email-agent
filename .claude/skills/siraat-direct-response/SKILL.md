# Siraat direct response copy

Compact version of `research/copy/05-siraat-copy-playbook.md`. Background and evidence: `research/copy/01-dr-research.md` (principles, influence, social proof inventory, 15 case-study reviews, Email Love), `02-awareness-map.md` (per mail: awareness level, job, length, principles), `03-copy-audit.md` (scores of the 31 v3 mails), `04-rewrites.md` (ready copy per mail), `06-offer-design.md` (value equation, value stack, guarantees, urgency, pricing). Original frameworks: `research/copy/direct-response-copy-skill.md`, `influence-based-copywriting.md`, `social-proof-copywriting.md`, `offers-skill.md`.
Priority on conflict: DECISIONS.md > PLAYBOOK.md > this skill. Notes to Siraat in Dutch, email copy in English (US).

## 1. Voice
- A smart friend explaining something he figured out. Skeptical-friendly, specific, never hyped. The reader is smart and has thrown out a pan before.
- Benjamin is the founder and the only "I". Never Floris or Ali.
- Flows: calm (sentence case, no exclamation marks, numbers over adjectives). Campaigns may be loud, same fact rules.
- One email, one idea, one messaging pillar.
- Rhythm: short. Then a longer sentence that explains and breathes. Then short.
- Contractions, "I" and "you". One honest limitation where it fits ("Skip the heat, and it sticks.").
- If a sentence could come from any DTC brand, sharpen it until only Siraat could say it.

## 2. What only we can say
1. Nothing on it to wear off: a pure titanium cooking surface, no coatings. That is why there is a 75-year warranty.
2. The paper, not the label: Light Labs report no. 25895, 31 PFAS compounds tested, every one below the detection limit, ISO/IEC 17025-accredited lab, released October 30, 2025. A competitor uses the same lab, so the number, the 31 and "read it yourself" are the difference.
3. The original hammered titanium pan, first orders shipped December 20, 2024. Look-alikes came after (never name a competitor).
Reason-why: no coating, so release comes from heat and timing. "Cold metal grabs your food, hot metal lets it go." Medium heat 2 to 3 minutes, the water drop forms a ball and glides, then a teaspoon of oil.

## 3. Claims
Allowed: with a pure titanium cooking surface + no coatings (always together) · No PFAS, PFOA, or BPA · Tested free from PFAS by Light Labs, report no. 25895 (tested product Pan Pro; other pans: "the same titanium cooking surface") · 75-year warranty against defects (dent, warped base, loose handle or rivet, lid that no longer fits) · 30-day returns (unused, within 30 days of delivery) · Free shipping on all orders (INT: duties paid) · 100,000+ happy customers · The original hammered titanium pan, first orders December 20, 2024 · dishwasher safe, metal utensil safe, oven safe (no temperature), induction/gas/electric/ceramic, no seasoning · 3-ply (Pan Pro only) · anti-microbial (cutting board only).
Never: lifetime warranty · 100-day trial · risk-free · money-back · free returns (return shipping on unused items is the customer's) · scratch-proof · non-stick as a headline · layer thickness in mm · oven temperature · "30 of 30 tests" · health or medical claims · competitor names · "unsolicited" or "independent" about reviews · "free filter" or "purifier" (it is "a chance to win a PFAS water filter") · delivery times in marketing emails · "the handle stays cool" · fake urgency (reserved, price held, low stock, countdown without a real end).
Unsure: check `content/facts/claims.csv` and `facts.csv`. Not there: do not write it, or mark [VRAAG FLORIS].
Founder story: only what DECISIONS, PLAYBOOK and brand say (Benjamin, Dutch founders, started with titanium cutting boards, the original hammered pan, first orders December 20, 2024, 100,000+ customers in a little over a year). Anything else is [VRAAG FLORIS].

## 4. Awareness sets headline and length (Schwartz)
| Reader | Where | Headline is about | Length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Most aware (5) | checkout, cart, last emails | their cart, one doubt, the offer | 60 to 180 words |
| Product aware (4) | browse, W5, B2 | the difference (mechanism) or proof (story) | 120 to 230 |
| Solution aware (3) | W3, W4, B1 | why this pan, the three questions, the size | 150 to 260 |
| Problem aware (2) | W1, W2 | the problem or the idea, not the discount | 200 to 350 |
| Customer | post-purchase, winback | their next moment (first egg, the lid) | short, P2 longer |
The offer lives in the code bar and the hero offer bar (PLAYBOOK 11). The hero headline carries the email's one idea. Only the last email with a unique code may lead with the offer.

## 5. Headlines and subject lines
Always write 5 subject lines, one per type, then pick 2 for the A/B:
- Curiosity + benefit: "The one mistake that makes titanium stick" (never curiosity alone, always close the loop)
- Specificity: "Report no. 25895", "December 20, 2024", "15 pans, or one."
- Question: "Has your cookware actually been tested?", "Who are you cooking for?", "Something stop you at checkout?"
- Story (verbatim customer quote only): "\"I only need the new one.\"", "Tammy is on her fourth pan"
- Offer: "Your own 10%, for 48 hours"
A/B: one variable. Prefer a principle test (social proof vs authority, scarcity vs liking) over appending "(+10% off)". Winner on unique clicks, then revenue per recipient. Short and specific often wins (under 21 characters), long is fine when specific.
Hero format: LABEL (1 to 5 words, caps) / `Line1|Line2` (max 2 short lines) / subline (max ~45 characters).
Openers: short ("Every pan says non-toxic. Few can show you the paper."), their thought ("Something stopped you?"), the math ("Say a good coated pan lasts 5 years..."), honest ("I'll be honest about one thing."). Never "Welcome! We're so glad you're here."

## 6. Influence principles (at least two per email)
| Principle | What we really have | Our words | Not our words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reciprocity | 4 gifts ($15 shipping, $30 e-book, $25 mystery gift, chance at $450 filter), chef guide, the three questions | included, yours, with every order, here is | FREE!!!, no strings, unlock |
| Commitment | size choice, first egg, reply question, their choice for no coating | you chose, pick, start with, your first, which one | journey, transform |
| Social proof | 100,000+ happy customers, verified reviews, repeat buyers | 100,000+ happy customers, verified buyer, in their words, her fourth pan | thousands of, trending |
| Authority | Light Labs, ISO/IEC 17025, report no. 25895, the chef | tested, report no., accredited lab, read it yourself | clinically proven, doctor recommended |
| Scarcity | unique codes (48 h, 72 h, 14 days), last email of a series | your own code, ends 48 hours after this email, then the regular sale price | hurry, only X left, reserved, last chance (unless truly last) |
| Unity | people who cook without coatings | the no-coating kitchen, our regulars | tribe, fam |
| Liking | Benjamin, honest notes, real replies | I, honest note, reply and tell me | bestie, obsessed |
Scarcity must always be true. Every deadline gets a reason ("made for you, so it has an end date") and what happens after ("then the regular sale price applies"). HI10 never gets a deadline. The weekly filter draw is not a deadline until official rules exist.

## 7. Social proof
1. Match proof to the claim (warranty email: R240 Brandon W.; set upsell: R025 Sunny C.; cleaning: R518 Graham C.).
2. Story over praise: before, action, outcome, emotion. "Great pan" does not count.
3. Spread it: one line right under the cart or headline, the review block later, a case block where doubt is highest.
4. Specific numbers: "100,000+", "her 4th pan", "31 PFAS".
5. Mix peer and expert: every sales email has at least one customer and one authority.
6. Never fake. Never write or edit review text. Only select and shorten with "..."; keep typos. The review block shows 5 stars, so 5-star reviews only. Max 120 characters, pair difference max 30. Verify: `python3 -I research/copy/check_reviews.py`.
7. Skip reviews that mention the old policy (100-day, lifetime) unless that part is cut, or that name a competitor.
Shortlist of 15 case-study reviews with best mail per review: `01-dr-research.md` section 5.

## 8. Offer, CTA, friction
- Button: first person with a benefit ("Complete my order", "Claim my 10% + gifts", "Get 10% off this pan", "Upgrade to the 6-piece set"). Never "Shop now", "Learn more", "Buy now".
- Under every primary button a friction reducer (risk reversal + social proof + ease):
  - US: `Free shipping. 30-day returns. 100,000+ happy customers.`
  - With code: `One use, already applied. 30-day returns. 100,000+ happy customers.`
  - INT: `Free shipping, duties paid. 30-day returns. 100,000+ happy customers.`
- Price: anchor, price, price after code in dollars: `$439  $134  $120.60 with HI10` (Standard 11" only; other sizes have no compare-at). Sets: per pan ("$349, about $116 a pan, lid included"). Say "covered for 75 years", never "lasts 75 years".
- Value stack in sales emails per `06-offer-design.md`: Pan Pro $439 value, $15 + $30 + $25 included, warranty and returns included, code, "Value $509 / Your price $120.60". The $450 filter is "chance to win", never added to the total.
- Last email of a flow: full risk reversal, "Two promises": changed your mind (unused, 30 days, full refund) and something wrong (damaged or defective in 30 days: refund or replacement, we pay shipping; then the 75-year warranty).
- Keep the offer and the PLAYBOOK 11 block order intact: HI10, $70 in gifts, unique codes with their real terms.

## 9. AI-tells and banned words
- Never an em dash (the long dash, U+2014), not even in examples. Use a period or a comma.
- Banned: delve, dive into, comprehensive, robust, cutting-edge, utilize, leverage, crucial, vital, essential, unlock, unleash, supercharge, game-changer, revolutionary, landscape, navigate, streamline, elevate, seamless, curated, must-have, journey, "In today's fast-paced world", "It's important to note", "When it comes to", "Whether you're a... or a...", "take your X to the next level", "Look no further", "Don't miss out".
- Our own tics to ration: "Just reply. A real person reads every email." (max 3 emails per series), "Nothing to X, nothing to Y", "No coating. Ever.", colon sublines, "on us", "no catch", "just for you".
- No same-length paragraphs, no identical bullet openings, no bold on every key term, no hedging ("may help", "can potentially").

## 10. The test (read it out loud before shipping)
1. Does it sound like someone talking?
2. Would Benjamin say this to a friend?
3. Could every sentence only be Siraat's?
4. Does the headline fit this reader's awareness level, with one idea (not "10% off" again)?
5. Is every claim in claims.csv or facts.csv, with a number or proof?
6. At least two influence principles, and is scarcity true, with reason and what-after?
7. Is proof matched to the claim and verbatim (script run)?
8. Every button: benefit plus friction reducer?
9. Rhythm alternates, every open loop closes?
10. Five subject lines written, A/B is one variable?
11. No em dash, no banned word, no "free returns", no "lifetime", no delivery times.
If any answer is no, rewrite that part.

## 11. Workflow
1. Read the mail's row in `research/copy/02-awareness-map.md` (level, job, length, principles).
2. Check `content/facts/claims.csv`, `facts.csv`, DECISIONS.md for every fact.
3. Pick proof from the shortlist in `01-dr-research.md` section 5 or from `content/reviews/reviews.csv`; run the review check.
4. Write 5 subject lines, preview, hero (label, `Line1|Line2`, subline), opener, body, button, friction reducer.
5. Run the test in section 10. Then `grep -c $'\u2014' <file>` must return 0.
6. Do not change email HTML or anything in Klaviyo, Shopify or Figma without Floris's go-ahead.
