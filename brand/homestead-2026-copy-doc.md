# **Strategy & Concepts**

## **Monthly Strategy Summary**

In a few sentences, explain your overall campaign strategy for the month. 

  

September splits into two arcs. The adjustment window (through 9/15) reuses the account's most successful sends, grounded in 7/15–8/30 performance data with subject lines, preview text, and headers refreshed to maintain cadence and content consistency. This allows your customers to slowly adjust to updated brandfeel through copy before net-new visuals are launched. 

The Warehouse Clearance Sale (9/9–9/30, value-led per client direction) is the month's primary cash-flow driver ("Stacktember"). From 9/17, we launch our full creative direction, with net-new Mediterranean-inspired creative debuts, anchored by the Roasting Pan launch (US-only, full price — excluded from clearance discount, benefit-led per client direction) and closing with early Q4 SMS-growth and gift-guide-teaser sends ahead of BFCM. Sale banners will appear on most content sends through the Warehouse Clearance Sale period to drive revenue throughout the month-long sale, aside from important focus moments like the roasting pan launch.

In total: 15 emails and 9 SMS sends   
(leaving room for 5 email and 3 SMS for revamped Welcome Flow)
