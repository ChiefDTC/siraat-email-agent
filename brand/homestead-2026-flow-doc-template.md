**Strategic Overview**

**AUDIENCE:** Who are they and what do they want? 

  

**HERO PRODUCT(S):** Which products are purchased by first-time customers most often? Why? What about repeat customers?

  

**BEHAVIOR:** What is the average time from signup to first purchase? What about from first purchase to second purchase? 

  

**POTENTIAL OBJECTIONS:** What product or brand attributes are keeping them from purchasing? (Price, competition, lack of education, etc.)

  

**OFFER:** What is the welcome offer? How is the discount applied?  

  

**LEARNINGS:** Which creative learnings have we gathered from the audit and/or through A/B testing that will help us make the maximum impact?

# Replenishment  

  

**Replenishment**  

  
  
  

|  |  |
| :-: | :-: |
| \*\*Trigger:\*\* Placed order x days ago | \*\*Description:\*\* Customers are most likely to buy the same thing they’ve bought before. Time up this flows trigger to send emails about re purchasing around their most likely time. Give an offer to subscribe after the first email, then remind the discount expires. Could test multiple emails in here as plain text. |
| \*\*Flow Filters:\*\* Placed order zero times since joining flow | \*\*Notes:\*\* Ask client to download the lifetimely app and start the pro trial. We will want to see how long it takes someone to reorder their product and then customize our time delay based off that. |

  
  
  
**  
**

  - **\*\*Content Suggestions\*\***  
      - Running low on xyz?
      - Ready to re-order X?
      - Keep the benefits going
      - Talk about how they might be feeling using xyz consistently
      - Here’s a discount to subscribe
      - Discount reminder

|  |  |
| :-: | :-: |

# Birthday Flow  

  

**Birthday Flow**  

  
  
  

|  |  |
| :-: | :-: |
| \*\*Trigger:\*\* 1\.  Customer birthday is in the next 14 days  | \*\*Description:\*\* Invite a customer to get themselves a birthday gift 14-30 days before their submitted birthday, utilizing email/SMS touchpoints.  |
| \*\*Flow Filters:\*\* Has placed order 0 times since joining flow.   | \*\*Notes:\*\*We’re inviting a customer to get themselves a birthday gift with a small discount. Customers will be removed from the flow if they place an order. As a rule of thumb, would want this offer to be less strong than the welcome offer. Reccs on birthday collection: Pop-up (or side bar) for customers who have already signed up for emails and are returning to site. Copy can vary based on brand and product guidelines, but thematically will be asking a customer if they want a birthday gift. “Do you want a birthday treat?” with a yes/no CTA with and a follow-up form to submit a birthday. This should feed into a Klaviyo birthday field.  |

  
  

  - **\*\*Content Suggestions\*\***  
      - Personalization where possible - particularly using first name 
      - For birthday, we want the first message to hit at least 14 days before birthday so that it’s still relevant + increase touchpoints.
      - For half-birthday, one touchpoint for email/SMS (less planned than a birthday and it becomes less relevant the further you are away from the “half-birthday” date.
      - Will want to include an offer on these to act as the gift - “treat yourself”, “because you deserve it” kind of messaging. Ex.. “You deserve to feel special more than once a year”. For half-birthday and “Celebrating your special day” for birthday.

**Example:**

  

  
  
  
  

**Strategy**

  

**Email 1: We miss you\!**

|  |
| :-: |
| \*\*Timing\*\*: 25 days after cancellation\*\*Content\*\*: "It looks like you’ve been missing out on your results\\\! Your fridge must be empty. Reactivate now and get back to feeling great." Tailor the messaging to their cancellation reason (e.g., too much product, remind them of the value and offer a reactivation link with a discount baked in).If we can pull the cancellation reason from the profile property that would be the best option here. Ideally, we’re not sending to people who said it was too expensive in most cases.\*\*Objective\*\*: Highlight what the customer is missing and drive reactivation through value-based messaging.\*\*Key Metric:\*\* CTR and PoR |
