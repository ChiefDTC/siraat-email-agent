How to get the perfect release
1

Preheat on medium, 2–3 min. A drop of water should bead and glide across the surface.

2

Add a thin layer of oil. It shimmers when the pan is ready for food.

3

Let it sear, then move it. Food releases on its own once a crust forms — don't rush it.
