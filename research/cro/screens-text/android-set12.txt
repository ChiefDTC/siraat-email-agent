How to get the perfect release
1

Preheat over medium for a minute — let the pan come up to temperature.

2

Add a little fat — oil or butter — and let it shimmer.

3

Cook, then let it release — food loosens on its own when it’s ready to flip.

Why traditional pans are the past
PFAS / TEFLON

Nonstick coatings scratch, flake, and break down over time. When overheated they can release fumes that may increase health risks. Every couple of years, you buy another one.

STAINLESS STEEL

Safe and tough, but everything sticks. It demands constant oil, careful heat control, and hard scrubbing — and still leaves burnt-on messes behind.

CERAMIC

Sold as the natural nonstick, but the coating wears thin within months. Once the release is gone, the pan is done — and back to the landfill it goes.

Inside every piece
HAMMERED PATTERN TECHNOLOGY
Natural release, no coating

The pans' hammered surface creates thousands of micro-ridges that reduce contact between food and metal. Eggs slide, fish flips clean, and the release comes from the titanium itself — never from a coating that can wear off.

TRI-PLY CONSTRUCTION · PURE TITANIUM COOKING SURFACE
Three layers, engineered to cook

A fully-clad build with a pure titanium cooking surface bonded to an aluminum heat core and a stainless steel base. Even heat, no hot spots, and a toxin-free surface that never leaches into food.
