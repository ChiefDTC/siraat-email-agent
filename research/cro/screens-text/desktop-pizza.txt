OVERVIEW

The Titanium Hammered Pizza Steel is engineered for high-heat pizza baking, delivering fast, even heat for crisp, well-browned crusts. Crafted from pure, food-grade titanium, the hammered surface improves airflow for easy release after baking. It will not crack, absorb odors, or degrade over time and contains no coatings or toxins.
