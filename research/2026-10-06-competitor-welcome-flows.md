# Competitor welcome flows (Email Love), 6 October 2026

Source: Email Love search_emails and 10 HTML pulls. No curated journeys exist for cookware brands, so timing is inferred from send dates.

## Findings per brand
- HexClad: 4 emails (day 0, +3, +3, +5). E1 welcome + tiered credit + 6-tile bestseller grid; E2 "Why folks love us" education + 30% set; E3 Gordon Ramsay social proof; E4 founder letter (last, short) + offer. All image slices, offer in every email.
- Made In: 1 welcome, "We're excited to have you back at our table." Heritage story, live text, one hero, no grid, no discount. CTAs "COOK MORE", "VISIT THE STUDIO". Cleanest no-discount welcome.
- Our Place: "Welcome! You've made the Guest list." Loyalty-club framing instead of a code. Education format "The Always Pan 101": annotated hero diagram with 7 callouts.
- Le Creuset: free shipping code, immediately. All-Clad: 10% off, immediately, lifetime guarantee block. Great Jones: 10% "housewarming gift", code expires in 12 days.
- Brooklinen: story, trust strip (150,000+ reviews, 365-day warranty), "What We're Most Famous For" two products with review quotes.
- Crate & Barrel: 2 emails one day apart, code first, editorial second.
- Hestan giveaway: "You're in" confirmation, itemised prize value, "Every Entry Brings You Closer" bonus-entry checklist (email, SMS, follow, share, quiz).

## Pattern
Day 0 instant; +1 to +3; +3; +5. Trust strip nearly everywhere. Bestsellers with stars in mail 1 or 2. Education second. Founder letter late and short. Premium brands use live text and one hero; mass DTC uses image slices.

## Best subject lines seen
Welcome! You've made the Guest list. · A letter from HexClad's founder · Why folks love us · The Always Pan 101 · Our Biggest Giveaway EVER · 3 Tasks This Knife Was Made For · Welcome to the insider list
Preview lines: "Lovely to meet you, Chef!" · "(They really love us.)" · "We're excited to have you back at our table."
